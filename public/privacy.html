Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Narsil is a digital museum and art browsing app. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Narsil, including when you sign in with Google.

Who we are

Narsil is operated at the contact address below. Questions about this policy: narsil.business@gmail.com

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in. If you continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and public profile information so we can create or sign in to your Narsil account.
  • Account data. Email, display name, and account settings are stored with our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • App activity. Saved art, likes, follows, quiz progress, and similar in-app choices associated with your account.
  • Purchases. If you buy a canvas print or subscribe, payment is processed by Stripe or Apple. We receive order status, not your full card number.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, sync your library across devices, provide support, process orders, and improve Narsil. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data with service providers who help us operate Narsil (authentication, hosting, payments) and when required by law. Google sign-in is governed also by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account by emailing narsil.business@gmail.com.
